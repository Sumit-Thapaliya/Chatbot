4. Product Knowledge
4.1 Overview
KB-OVR-01 | The School ERP is a modular platform for managing school operations: students and
admissions, academics, attendance, fees and finance, examinations, staff, communication, library,
transport and more. It serves several user types: school administrators, teachers, parents and students,
and, for platform operators, a super-admin console.
KB-OVR-02 | The platform supports multiple schools and branches on shared infrastructure with strict
data isolation. Schools configure their own grading, fees, calendars and templates instead of using
custom code.
KB-OVR-03 | Modules are released in phases. Phase 1: foundation, students, academics, attendance,
fees, exams and basic reports. Phase 2: staff, parent and student portals, homework/LMS,
communication, library, transport. Phase 3: advanced accounting, HR and payroll, inventory, hostel,
health, events. Later phases add advanced analytics, automation, AI-assisted insights and enterprise
SaaS features.
KB-OVR-04 | Security foundations include login history, optional two-factor authentication, role-based
permissions, audit logs, encrypted data and regular backups.
4.2 Glossary
ID Term Meaning
KB-GLO-01 Tenant One school or school group using the platform. Each tenant's data is fully
isolated from every other tenant.
KB-GLO-02 Branch A campus under a tenant. Some records and permissions can be limited to
a branch.
KB-GLO-03 Academic session An academic year (for example 2026-27), optionally split into terms. Most
academic records belong to a session.
KB-GLO-04 Term A division of an academic session, such as Term 1 or Term 2.
KB-GLO-05 Grade A class level, for example Grade 5. Called 'class' in some schools.
KB-GLO-06 Section A division of a grade, for example A, B or C.
KB-GLO-07 Enrollment The placement of a student in a specific section for a specific session.
Attendance, fees and marks attach to the enrollment.
KB-GLO-08 Admission The application process that ends with a student being accepted, rejected
or waitlisted.
KB-GLO-09 Registration number A unique student identifier generated by the system at admission.
KB-GLO-10 Guardian A parent or authorised adult linked to a student. A guardian can be linked to
several children.
KB-GLO-11 Fee category A type of charge, such as tuition, admission, exam, transport or library.
KB-GLO-12 Fee structure The set of charges and installment schedule that applies to a grade,
session or category.
KB-GLO-13 Invoice A bill generated for a student from their assigned fee plan.
KB-GLO-14 Installment One scheduled part of a fee (monthly, quarterly, termly or annual).
KB-GLO-15 Partial payment A payment smaller than the amount due; the balance stays outstanding.
KB-GLO-16 Concession /
scholarship
An approved reduction applied to a student's fees.

ID Term Meaning
KB-GLO-17 Fine A late-payment charge calculated by the school's configured policy.
KB-GLO-18 Refund Money returned to a payer after approval.
KB-GLO-19 Reversal / adjustment A controlled correction entry. Financial records are never silently edited;
they are reversed or adjusted with a visible history.
KB-GLO-20 Receipt Proof of a payment, printable or downloadable.
KB-GLO-21 Ledger The full list of a student's charges and payments.
KB-GLO-22 Marks entry Recording each student's marks per subject and assessment component.
KB-GLO-23 Grading scale The school's mapping from marks to grades or grade points, including pass
criteria.
KB-GLO-24 Result processing Calculation of totals, percentages and grades from entered marks.
KB-GLO-25 Result approval A review step (usually by the principal) before results can be published.
KB-GLO-26 Publication Making approved results visible to students and parents.
KB-GLO-27 Report card A formatted document of a student's results for a term or session.
KB-GLO-28 RBAC Role-based access control. Users get roles; roles hold permissions.
KB-GLO-29 Permission An allowed action in a module: create, read, update, delete, approve, export
or assign.
KB-GLO-30 Scope The limit of a permission: whole school, one branch, one class, or assigned
records only.
KB-GLO-31 Audit log A record of who changed what and when.
KB-GLO-32 2FA Two-factor authentication: a second verification step at login.
KB-GLO-33 Entitlement Whether a module is enabled for a school under its subscription plan.
KB-GLO-34 Promotion Moving students to the next grade at the end of a session according to
configured rules.
KB-GLO-35 BS / AD Bikram Sambat (Nepali) calendar and the Gregorian (AD) calendar. Dates
are stored in AD and can be displayed in BS.

4.5 Business rules
ID Rule What the bot must do
KB-RULE-01 Tenant isolation A user can never see or change another school's data. The bot must
never reference data from outside the user's school.
KB-RULE-02 Role and scope Answers about records must respect the user's role and scope. A
teacher sees only assigned classes; a parent sees only linked children.
KB-RULE-03 Enrollment-based records Attendance, fees and marks are tied to a student's enrollment in a
session, not just the student.
KB-RULE-04 History is preserved Student, staff and academic history is kept. Promotions, transfers and
changes add history; they do not overwrite it.
KB-RULE-05 No silent financial edits Payments and invoices are corrected only by reversal or adjustment with
a reason, approver and trail. They are never edited or deleted.
KB-RULE-06 Attendance corrections Correcting attendance requires permission and a reason, and is logged.

ID Rule What the bot must do
KB-RULE-07 Results workflow Results move through marks entry, processing, approval and publication.
Unpublished results are not visible to parents or students.
KB-RULE-08 Approval for sensitive
actions
Refunds, result approval and permission changes may require explicit
approval.
KB-RULE-09 Configurable policies Grading scales, fee fines, installment plans, attendance rules and
templates are set per school. The bot must not state one school's policy
as universal.
KB-RULE-10 Module entitlement A module is available only if the school's plan includes it and the user's
role has permission.
KB-RULE-11 Sensitive data Health, welfare and counselling records are highly restricted and access
is logged. The bot must not reveal or summarise them.
KB-RULE-12 Audit Important actions (financial transactions, permission changes, record
changes) are logged.
KB-RULE-13 Notifications Parent alerts and reminders are sent only if the school has enabled them
and a provider is configured.
KB-RULE-14 Phased release Features are released in phases. The bot must say a feature is planned
or unavailable if it is not live for that school.

5. How-to Guides
Steps follow the proposed screen specification. Replace menu names with final labels when the interface
is built.
KB-HOW-01 · Submit an admission application
Role: Parent / applicant   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the school's public admission page.
2. Fill in the applicant, parent or guardian and previous-school details.
3. Upload the required documents.
4. Review the summary and submit.
5. Note the reference number shown; use it to track the status.
Note: Application statuses: New, In review, Approved, Rejected, Waitlisted.
KB-HOW-02 · Review and approve an admission
Role: Admission officer   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Students > Admissions.
2. Open an application from the New or In review tab.
3. Check details and documents; add notes if needed.
4. Choose Approve, Reject or Waitlist.
5. On approval, choose Convert to student and pick the grade and section.
Note: Converting creates the student record, a registration number and an enrollment.
KB-HOW-03 · Add a student manually
Role: Admission officer / school admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Students > Student list and choose Add student.
2. Complete the steps: personal, family, contact, previous school, documents.
3. Add at least one guardian.
4. Save. The registration number is generated automatically.
Note: Use Save draft to finish later.
KB-HOW-04 · Import students in bulk
Role: School admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Students > Bulk import.
2. Download the template and fill it in.
3. Upload the file and map columns if asked.
4. Review the validation report and fix flagged rows.
5. Confirm the import.
Note: Rows with errors are skipped and can be downloaded for correction.
KB-HOW-05 · Enroll or promote students
Role: School admin / principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Students > Enrollment & promotion.
2. Select the source grade and section, and the target session, grade and section.
3. Review the rule preview and tick the students.
4. Choose Promote, Retain or Transfer and confirm.
Note: Promotion follows the school's configured rules; history is preserved.
KB-HOW-06 · Process a transfer or withdrawal
Role: School admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the student's profile.
2. Choose Transfer / withdraw.
3. Select reason and effective date.

4. Complete the clearance checklist (fees, library).
5. Confirm; optionally generate a certificate.
Note: The student's history is kept, not deleted.
KB-HOW-07 · Set up a session, grades and sections
Role: School admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Academics > Academic sessions and create the session with its terms.
2. Go to Grades & sections and add grades, then sections with capacity.
3. Go to Subjects and map subjects to grades.
4. Activate the session.
Note: A setup wizard guides new schools through this order.
KB-HOW-08 · Assign a teacher to a class and subject
Role: School admin / principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Academics > Teacher assignment.
2. Find the section and subject in the matrix.
3. Choose the teacher from the dropdown.
4. Save.
Note: Workload per teacher is visible after assignment.
KB-HOW-09 · Build the timetable
Role: School admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Academics > Timetable.
2. Choose the section.
3. Drag subject and teacher cards into period slots.
4. Resolve any clash warnings (same teacher or room, same period).
5. Publish.
Note: Clashes block publishing until fixed.
KB-HOW-10 · Mark daily attendance
Role: Teacher   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Attendance > Mark attendance.
2. Select class, section and date.
3. Use Mark all present, then change exceptions to Absent, Late or Leave.
4. Save.
Note: Parent alerts for absences are sent if the school has enabled them.
KB-HOW-11 · Correct an attendance record
Role: Teacher with permission / admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Attendance > Corrections.
2. Search the student and date.
3. Change the status and enter a reason.
4. Submit.
Note: A reason is mandatory and the change is recorded in the audit history.
KB-HOW-12 · Approve a leave request
Role: Class teacher / principal / HR   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Attendance > Leave requests.
2. Open the request.
3. Choose Approve or Reject and add a comment.
4. Confirm.
Note: Approved leave appears as Leave in attendance.

KB-HOW-13 · Create a fee structure and assign it
Role: Accountant   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Finance > Fee categories & structures and create categories.
2. Create a fee structure by grade and session with the installment schedule.
3. Go to Assign fees, select students or a class and the plan.
4. Add any discount or scholarship.
5. Assign, then generate invoices.
Note: Clone last year's structure to save time.
KB-HOW-14 · Collect a fee payment
Role: Accountant   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Finance > Collection counter.
2. Search the student by name or ID.
3. Select the invoices to pay.
4. Enter amount and payment mode; add discount or fine lines if applicable.
5. Collect and print the receipt.
Note: The ledger updates immediately.
KB-HOW-15 · Accept a partial payment
Role: Accountant   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the Collection counter and select the invoice.
2. Enter an amount lower than the due amount.
3. Collect; the remaining balance stays outstanding.
Note: More payments can be added later against the same invoice.
KB-HOW-16 · Correct or refund a payment
Role: Accountant with approver   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Finance > Refunds & adjustments.
2. Select the original payment.
3. Enter the reason and the refund or adjustment amount.
4. Submit for approval.
5. After approval, the reversal entry is posted.
Note: Original entries are never edited or deleted.
KB-HOW-17 · Create an exam and schedule it
Role: Principal / exam coordinator   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Examinations > Exam list and choose Create.
2. Set exam type, classes and weightage, and add theory and practical components.
3. Open Exam schedule and add date, time and room per subject.
4. Publish the schedule.
Note: Schedule publication notifies students and parents if notifications are on.
KB-HOW-18 · Enter marks
Role: Teacher   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Examinations > Marks entry.
2. Select exam, class and subject.
3. Type marks in the grid; use codes for absent or exempt.
4. Fix any out-of-range warnings.
5. Save, then Lock when final.
Note: Marks can also be imported from Excel.
KB-HOW-19 · Approve and publish results
Role: Principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Examinations > Result processing and run Calculate.

2. Resolve any missing-marks flags.
3. Send for approval.
4. In Result approval, review and choose Approve.
5. Choose Publish.
Note: Results become visible to parents and students only after publication.
KB-HOW-20 · Generate report cards
Role: School admin / principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Examinations > Report cards.
2. Choose exam or term, class and template.
3. Preview one card.
4. Generate; download or print when ready.
Note: Large batches run in the background and appear in the Downloads centre.
KB-HOW-21 · Publish a notice
Role: School admin / principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Communication > Notice board and choose New notice.
2. Write the notice and attach files if needed.
3. Choose the audience (whole school, class, role).
4. Publish now or schedule.

KB-HOW-22 · Send an SMS or email to a class
Role: School admin / teacher with permission   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Communication > Compose message.
2. Choose the channel.
3. Select the audience, for example Grade 5 Section A.
4. Write the message or pick a template.
5. Check the recipient preview and send.
Note: Delivery status is visible in Communication history.
KB-HOW-23 · Add a user and assign a role
Role: School owner / admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Settings > Users and choose Invite user.
2. Enter name and email or phone.
3. Select one or more roles and a branch if needed.
4. Send the invitation.
Note: The user sets their own password from the invitation link.
KB-HOW-24 · Create a custom role
Role: School owner   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Go to Settings > Roles & permissions.
2. Choose Create role or clone an existing one.
3. Tick permissions per module (create, read, update, delete, approve, export, assign).
4. Set the scope and save.
Note: Grant the least access needed.
KB-HOW-25 · Reset a password
Role: Any user / admin   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. On the login page choose Forgot password.
2. Enter your email or phone.
3. Open the link sent to you and set a new password.
Note: Admins can also trigger a reset from Settings > Users.

KB-HOW-26 · View a child's attendance and results
Role: Parent   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the parent app or portal and choose the child at the top.
2. Tap Attendance for the monthly calendar.
3. Tap Results to see published marks and download report cards.
Note: Only published results are shown.
KB-HOW-27 · Pay a fee online
Role: Parent   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open Fees & payments for the child.
2. Select the due invoice.
3. Choose Pay now and select a payment provider.
4. Complete payment; download the receipt.
Note: Available only if the school has enabled online payments.
KB-HOW-28 · Apply for student leave
Role: Parent   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open Attendance for the child and choose Apply leave.
2. Enter dates and reason.
3. Submit; the class teacher reviews it.

KB-HOW-29 · Export a report
Role: Authorised users   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the report or list.
2. Apply filters.
3. Choose Export and select Excel, CSV or PDF.
Note: Export requires the export permission; large exports are delivered in the Downloads centre.
KB-HOW-30 · Check who changed a record
Role: School owner / principal   |   Status: PLANNED (verify labels)
1. Open the record and choose History, or go to Settings > Audit log.
2. Filter by user, module, action or date.
3. Open an entry to see before and after values.
Note: Only roles with audit access can view this.

6. FAQ Bank
Each entry is tagged with a role and topic so retrieval can be filtered. Answers are intentionally short and
safe.
KB-FAQ-001   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Product
Q: What is this system?
A: It is a School ERP: one platform for admissions, students, academics, attendance, fees, exams,
communication and reports, with role-specific screens for administrators, teachers, parents and students.
KB-FAQ-002   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Product
Q: Can one parent account see more than one child?
A: Yes. A parent account can be linked to several children and switch between them from the top of the
screen.
KB-FAQ-003   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Product
Q: Is my school's data visible to other schools?
A: No. Each school's data is isolated from all other schools.
KB-FAQ-004   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Login
Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?
A: On the login page choose Forgot password, enter your email or phone, and follow the link you
receive. If you get no message, ask your school administrator to trigger a reset.
KB-FAQ-005   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Login
Q: Why am I asked for a verification code?
A: Your school or role has two-factor authentication enabled for extra security. Enter the code sent to
you; use a backup code if you cannot receive it.
KB-FAQ-006   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Access
Q: Why can't I see a menu item my colleague can see?
A: Menus depend on your role and permissions, and on whether your school's plan includes that
module. Ask your school administrator to review your role.
KB-FAQ-007   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Access
Q: Who decides what I can do in the system?
A: Your school administrator assigns your roles. Each role has permissions such as create, read,
update, delete, approve, export and assign.
KB-FAQ-008   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Language
Q: Can I use the system in Nepali?
A: The system is designed for configurable language, date and currency formats, including Nepali and
BS dates, set in school configuration. Check with your school whether it is enabled.
KB-FAQ-009   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Attendance
Q: How do I mark attendance quickly?
A: Open Attendance > Mark attendance, choose class, section and date, press Mark all present, then
change only the absent, late or leave students and save.
KB-FAQ-010   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Attendance
Q: I marked a student wrong yesterday. Can I fix it?
A: Use Attendance corrections if you have permission. You must give a reason, and the change is
logged. If you lack permission, ask your class teacher or administrator.
KB-FAQ-011   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Attendance
Q: Do parents get a message when I mark a student absent?
A: Only if your school enabled absence alerts and has an SMS or notification provider configured.

KB-FAQ-012   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Exams
Q: Why can't I edit marks anymore?
A: Marks are probably locked after submission or the exam is in approval. Ask the exam coordinator or
principal to reopen it.
KB-FAQ-013   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Exams
Q: Can I upload marks from Excel?
A: Yes. Marks entry supports bulk import. Download the template, fill it, upload and fix any flagged rows.
KB-FAQ-014   |   Role: Teacher   |   Topic: Timetable
Q: Where can I see my timetable?
A: Your home screen and the Timetable view show your periods. Contact the administrator if a period is
missing.
KB-FAQ-015   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: A student is not showing any invoice. Why?
A: A fee plan may not be assigned or invoices may not be generated yet. Check Assign fees for the
student, then generate invoices.
KB-FAQ-016   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: Can I accept part of a fee?
A: Yes. Enter a lower amount at the Collection counter. The remaining balance stays outstanding and
further payments can be added.
KB-FAQ-017   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: I entered a wrong payment amount. How do I fix it?
A: Payments are not edited. Use Refunds & adjustments to post a reversal or adjustment with a reason;
it may need approval.
KB-FAQ-018   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: How is a late fine calculated?
A: By the school's configured fine policy. The rate and rules are set by your school, so check Finance
configuration or ask your administrator.
KB-FAQ-019   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: How do I find who has not paid?
A: Open Finance > Collection & dues reports and use the defaulter list, filtering by class or date. You can
export it or send reminders.
KB-FAQ-020   |   Role: Accountant   |   Topic: Fees
Q: Can siblings get a discount?
A: Sibling discounts are an advanced capability. If enabled by your school, they are set as a discount
rule in fee configuration.
KB-FAQ-021   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Students
Q: How do I admit a student who applied online?
A: Open Students > Admissions, review the application, choose Approve, then Convert to student and
pick the grade and section.
KB-FAQ-022   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Students
Q: How do I move students to the next class?
A: Use Enrollment & promotion: select source and target classes and sessions, review the rule preview
and confirm. History is preserved.
KB-FAQ-023   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Students
Q: Can I delete a student?
A: Students are normally transferred, withdrawn or marked inactive rather than deleted so that history is
preserved. Deletion, if available, requires a permission and is logged.

KB-FAQ-024   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Students
Q: My import file shows errors. What now?
A: Open the validation report, correct the flagged rows in your file, and upload again. Valid rows can be
imported while failed rows are fixed separately.
KB-FAQ-025   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Users
Q: How do I give someone access?
A: Go to Settings > Users, invite them, and assign a role. Create a custom role in Roles & permissions if
none fits.
KB-FAQ-026   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Users
Q: Can I limit a user to one branch or class?
A: Yes. Permissions can be scoped by branch, class or assigned responsibility.
KB-FAQ-027   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Settings
Q: Can we change the report card or receipt design?
A: Yes. Use Settings > Templates to edit and preview report card, receipt, certificate and ID card
templates.
KB-FAQ-028   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Settings
Q: Where can I see what changed on a record?
A: Open the record's History or go to Settings > Audit log and filter by user, module, action or date.
KB-FAQ-029   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Notifications
Q: Why did some messages fail?
A: Check Communication history for the failure reason. Common causes are invalid numbers or a
provider not configured. Test the provider in Settings > Integrations.
KB-FAQ-030   |   Role: Principal   |   Topic: Exams
Q: How do results get to parents?
A: Results are processed, sent for approval, approved and then published. Parents see them only after
publication.
KB-FAQ-031   |   Role: Principal   |   Topic: Reports
Q: Can I compare two campuses?
A: Yes, the dashboard's branch comparison and reports allow comparing branches where your role has
access.
KB-FAQ-032   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Attendance
Q: How do I see my child's attendance?
A: Choose your child at the top of the home screen and open Attendance for a monthly calendar and
percentage.
KB-FAQ-033   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Results
Q: Why can't I see my child's marks?
A: Results appear only after the school publishes them. If an exam is finished and results are still
missing, contact the school office.
KB-FAQ-034   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Fees
Q: How do I pay fees?
A: Open Fees & payments, select the invoice and choose Pay now, if your school has enabled online
payment. Otherwise pay at the school counter.
KB-FAQ-035   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Fees
Q: I paid but the invoice still shows due. What should I do?
A: Payments may take a short time to update. Keep your payment reference and contact the school
accounts office; the bot cannot confirm or alter payments.
KB-FAQ-036   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Leave
Q: How do I tell the school my child will be absent?
A: Open Attendance for the child and choose Apply leave. The class teacher will review it.

KB-FAQ-037   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Access
Q: My second child is not showing in my account.
A: The school must link the child to your guardian profile. Ask the school office to link them.
KB-FAQ-038   |   Role: Parent   |   Topic: Transport
Q: Where can I see the bus route?
A: Open Transport information for the child to see route, stop and driver contact, if your school uses
school transport.
KB-FAQ-039   |   Role: Student   |   Topic: Homework
Q: Where do I see my homework?
A: Your student dashboard lists homework with due dates. Open an item to read instructions and submit
work.
KB-FAQ-040   |   Role: Student   |   Topic: Results
Q: When will I see my result?
A: After your school approves and publishes it. You will see it on your dashboard and may receive a
notification.
KB-FAQ-041   |   Role: Library   |   Topic: Library
Q: How do I issue a book?
A: Open the Issue / return desk, scan or search the member, scan the book, confirm the due date and
issue.
KB-FAQ-042   |   Role: Transport   |   Topic: Transport
Q: How do I assign a student to a bus?
A: Go to Transport > Student assignment, choose the route, select the student and stop, and save.
Transport fees follow school configuration.
KB-FAQ-043   |   Role: Admin   |   Topic: Plans
Q: Why is a module greyed out or missing for our school?
A: The module may not be included in your subscription plan or may not be enabled yet. Contact your
platform provider to check entitlements.
KB-FAQ-044   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Data
Q: Can I download data?
A: Where your role has export permission you can export lists and reports to Excel, CSV or PDF. Large
exports appear in the Downloads centre.
KB-FAQ-045   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Privacy
Q: Who can see health information?
A: Only authorised health or welfare roles, with consent and access logging. The assistant cannot show
or discuss individual health records.
KB-FAQ-046   |   Role: All   |   Topic: Support
Q: The page is not loading or shows an error.
A: Refresh and try again. If it persists, note the page and time and contact your school administrator or
support.
